# 8. Non-blocking Floating Gamification Feedback & Haptic Loop

**Context & Decision**: 
Blocking popup dialogs after transaction saves interrupted continuous logging flow. We decided to transition to non-blocking floating XP gain toasts paired with tactile haptic feedback, reserving celebratory full modals exclusively for Level-Up milestones.

**Consequences**: 
- Frictionless batch or consecutive transaction entry.
- Immediate rewarding tactile feedback upon financial logging without requiring modal dismiss clicks.
